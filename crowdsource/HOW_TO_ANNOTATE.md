# How to help annotate (agentic coverage)

We are validating an AI-exposure research dataset. For each real work task you
judge **how much of it a capable autonomous AI agent (2026) could complete on its
own**, with no human stepping in to act, be physically present, decide, or take
responsibility. The agent can browse, write, reason, and use tools, but has no
physical body and no legal authority of its own.

One-task-at-a-time form (same content, slower pace): drop the `?view=grouped`.

## What to do

1. Open the link. Enter your **email first** — this is what saves your progress.
   If you have annotated before, your name is filled in automatically; if not,
   add your name so we can credit you.
2. For each task, move one slider from **0% to 100%**: how much of that task an
   autonomous agent could finish unaided. A short note is optional.
3. On the grouped form, set every slider for an occupation, then **Save this
   occupation** and move to the next.
4. You can stop anytime. Re-open the link with the **same email** and you resume
   exactly where you left off.

## Credit

Contributors are named in the **Acknowledgements** of the dataset and paper.
Credit is added **only for a full run**: all **180 occupations (200 tasks)**.
Partial runs still help the research, but only a complete run earns a credit.

## Notes

- The form is **blind by design**: you never see the penalty categories we are
  estimating, so ratings stay unbiased.
- Matching is by **email** (self-declared, not verified). Your name is only for
  the Acknowledgements. Please use the same email each time so your work resumes
  and does not duplicate.
- Two people rating the same tasks is welcome — overlap is how we measure
  inter-rater agreement.
